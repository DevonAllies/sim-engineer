Setup Engineer

Reads iRacing telemetry (.ibt), pulls the setup stored inside it, and suggests what to change.

Drop .ibt files here or click to choose
Usually in Documents\iRacing\telemetry. Press Alt+L in the car to record. Files stay on your computer.

Targets and assumptions

Ride-height targets for the Acura NSX GT3 come from iRacing's own setup notes (40 mm front / 55 mm rear dynamic, ±2.5 mm). Other cars start blank: fill them in from that car's setup notes.

Live

Each time you leave the pits a new stint starts (shown as a run chip). Analysis updates every lap. When you drive into the pit lane or back to the garage, the stint ends and the tyre temperatures are read.

For live mode and your recent sessions list, open Setup Engineer.exe instead of this file.
