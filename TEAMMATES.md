# Joining the Setup Engineer team

Hi! This gets you into our shared team folder, so we can plan team races together and learn from each other's laps.

## 1. Install Setup Engineer

- Download the latest `Setup-Engineer-Setup-x.y.z.exe` from
  https://github.com/DevonAllies/sim-engineer/releases/latest
- Close iRacing and run it. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed). Approve the admin prompt.
- From then on it updates itself (Settings → Updates shows the version and has a **Check for updates** button).

## 2. Install Google Drive for desktop

- https://www.google.com/drive/download/ — sign in with your Google account.

## 3. Join the team

- In Setup Engineer: **Settings → Learning → Team folder**: type your name, paste the **team key** from Discord (it starts with `SE1-`) in **Join a team**, and click **Join**.
- The first time, your browser opens the team folder: click **Add shortcut to Drive**. Google Drive syncs it, the app finds it by itself, and you're in.

## 4. Team races

- Open **Team events** (left menu). Our races are listed there: click **Join** on the ones you're in, **Not this one** on the others. Anyone can start an event.
- While you're in an event, your stints in that car at that track share compact laps and a stint summary (fuel, tyres, pace) into the event. Your `.ibt` files and the rest of your data stay on your PC.
- The event page shows everyone's fuel map, lift and coast corner by corner, and the start plan (stints, drivers, fuel at each stop). **Save the plan sheets** puts `plan.csv` and `fuel-map.csv` in the event folder for Google Sheets. Teammates' event laps appear in GP and Compare, so you can study their braking points.
- After the race you can remove your own data, and the event can be cleaned up.

## Testing cars for the app (optional)

- If you run a car programme (Test programme tab), you can switch on **Share these test results**. It shares only the settings you tested and what they did to the car — never your name, laps or telemetry — and it goes into the car knowledge in future updates for everyone.
